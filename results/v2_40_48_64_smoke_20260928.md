# 32q beam check and 40–64q probes

This report records the small scaling checks that came before the full [64q study](v2_64q_research_report_20260928.md). They used IBM fake targets locally, not a real QPU.

## Beam at 32q

On the same 120 cases, the 1-second beam attempt finished **0 routes**. Removing it left native 2Q count and 2Q depth unchanged in all 120 cases and reduced mean compile time from 1.875 s to 0.815 s. Beam is therefore skipped above 24 logical qubits; the 16–24q settings were kept. [Paired data](v2_32q_beam_ab_20260928.csv).

## Larger circuits

Each smoke test used medium50 and dense75 graphs with one and two QAOA layers on FakeBrisbane. The router passed symbolic replay, final-mapping, coupling, and native 2Q checks. No full statevector was built.

| Logical qubits | Hybrid routes | Peak process RAM |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 40 | 8/8 | 290 MiB |
| 48 | 8/8 | 331 MiB |
| 64 | 8/8 | 465 MiB |

A separate **four-case probe per size** compared Hybrid with Qiskit commuting + SAT. Hybrid won those 12 target cases, with 2Q savings of 9.7%/5.2% at 40q, 8.3%/4.3% at 48q, and 5.9%/3.4% at 64q for medium/dense graphs respectively. Four cases per size are too few to estimate a reliable win rate. The later 64q study used 120 fresh paired cases and should be used for the 64q conclusion. There is still no large paired benchmark at 40 or 48q.
